About PawConnect

PawConnect brings people and animal welfare organizations together to rescue, care for, and find loving homes for animals in need.

Our Mission

Make it easier for every concerned person to turn an animal sighting into meaningful help.

How PawConnect Works

Report an animal, connect with rescuers and veterinarians, follow the case, and help build a safer path home.

Why Animal Rescue Matters

Timely care can prevent suffering, reunite families, and give abandoned animals a second chance.

Our Community

Rescuers, veterinarians, volunteers, shelters, and adopters each play an essential role.

Contact

Have a question or partnership idea? Reach out through your local PawConnect team.
